# Bright papercraft presentation profile

This profile turns the fixed paper theatre into a bright editorial stage suited to AI education, workflow stories, evidence demonstrations, and open-source project explainers.

## Visual identity

- Use a warm ivory paper base, daylight blue sky, deep navy structure, warm orange emphasis, and restrained teal support colours.
- Build depth with paper thickness, cut edges, folded tabs, print grain, soft contact shadows, and physical stage mechanisms.
- Keep the main subject large and readable. Decorative objects support the semantic action and stop moving once their job is complete.
- Generate illustrated objects without exact text. Render Chinese text, numerals, repository names, URLs, labels, and semantic marks in post.
- Use familiar cultural archetypes only when rights and platform context allow. Public templates contain no copyrighted characters or private generated assets.

## Reusable scene families

1. **Paper frame machine** — explains frames, timing, assembly, and rendering.
2. **Asset workshop** — introduces characters, props, backgrounds, and their provenance as separate objects.
3. **Pop-up book assembly** — combines independent assets into one causal performance.
4. **Bright motion wall** — shows multiple completed results, then dims them behind one central statement.
5. **Error laboratory** — deliberately demonstrates a wrong target, then marks the corrected target and visible result.
6. **Workflow conveyor** — moves one task through registered steps; a focus ring must use measured target centres.
7. **Repository finale** — shows an exact Skill name and full repository URL with foreground protection and final-pixel review.

## Typography

- Use one primary statement per state, up to three lines.
- Emphasise one short phrase with orange; keep supporting copy smaller and lower contrast.
- Suspend the regular caption strip while a central hero statement is active.
- Register every glyph group as a protected object. No prop may cross its final glyph bounds plus safety padding.
- TTS-import captions preserve natural punctuation. On-screen captions follow the real audio and omit terminal punctuation unless the owner explicitly chooses another style.

## Semantic focus marks

A circle, underline, arrow, spotlight, or magnifier is an explanatory pointer. It must register `mark_id`, `target_id`, measured centres, tolerance, and final status in `semantic-focus.json`. Coordinates alone do not prove meaning; rendered pixels must still be reviewed at original size.

## Music

Background music is a sourced production asset. Record its exact path, SHA-256, provenance, render gain, audible review, and speech-masking result. Reusing a previous track means matching the exact file hash, not choosing a similar song by memory.
